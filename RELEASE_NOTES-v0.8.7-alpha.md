# Hearthroot Control v0.8.7-alpha

First public alpha candidate for POCO X7 Pro (`rodin`).

- Native, Balanced, Battery Saver, Performance and Experimental CPU/I/O profiles.
- Optional Companion v0.3.7-alpha (modern LSPosed) to synchronize Normal, Economy and Boost with Xiaomi Parts.
- Guarded bridge: CPU/I/O profiles continue to work when LSPosed or the Xiaomi Parts hook is unavailable.
- Apply on Boot support, including a fix for offline cpufreq policies in Xiaomi Parts Economy.
- Optional Companion install/update via Volume Up; Volume Down skips without uninstalling an existing app.
- Extreme mode and thermal bypass deliberately excluded.

**Test scope:** development POCO X7 Pro with Hearthroot Kernel, AOSP, BakaSU and Xiaomi Parts. Verify compatibility before installing on other configurations.
